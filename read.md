**Domain:** Energy Analytics and Electricity Consumption

### The Problem

Electricity consumption varies across locations, time periods, and consumer groups.

Understanding these variations is important for energy planning, demand management, and the efficient allocation of electricity resources.

This project explores high-resolution electricity consumption data from multiple Moroccan cities to identify consumption patterns and investigate differences in demand behaviour.
